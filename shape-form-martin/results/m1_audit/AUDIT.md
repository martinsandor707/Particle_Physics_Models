### 2.3 D7 — cross-validated score test for the MLP (`checks/b3_score_pass.py`, `b3_score_post.py`, `b3_score_sanity.py`)

**Family.** `log P_δ(c | p) = log P(c | p) + δ(c, p) − log Σ_c′ P(c′ | p) e^{δ(c′, p)}`,
`δ(c, p) = Σ_k h_k(ln p) δ[k, c]`, with hat functions h_k on momentum knots (they sum to 1). Main set: six knots at
0.5, 1.5, 4, 8, 13, 20 GeV (event weights 0.041, 0.085, 0.161, 0.227, 0.298, 0.188); also 1, 3 and 12 knots.

**Score.** The softmax normaliser cancels between the hit term and `M_e`, so with `u(c) = P(c) e^{δ(c)}`

    NLL_e(δ) = − Σ_i w_i ln[(1/K) Σ_j u(c_ij) / V(c_ij)] + W_e ln Σ_c m_e(c) u(c),        W_e = Σ_i w_i

where c_ij is the cell of fold point j of hit i and m_e(c) the fraction of the cell below `zb_e` (1, 0, or the linear
fraction in the z-bin of `zb_e`). Define

    q_i(c) = Σ_j 1[c_ij = c] ρ(c) / Σ_j ρ(c_ij)       responsibility of cell c for hit i (sums to 1)
    d_e(c) = Σ_i w_i q_i(c)                            energy of the event assigned to cell c by the model
    t_e(c) = P(c) m_e(c) / M_e                         model truncated at the event's back face

    ∂NLL_e/∂δ[k, c] = h_k(p_e) · [W_e t_e(c) − d_e(c)] =: h_k(p_e) G_e(c)

Checked against autograd through `sfm.metric.event_nll` on the first batch: max difference 2.4×10⁻⁷ (largest
component 0.36); Σ_c G_e(c) = 0 to 6×10⁻¹⁶.

**Gauge.** δ[k, ·] → δ[k, ·] + a_k leaves the map unchanged, and Σ_c g_k(c) = 0 for the mean gradient g_k. The gauge
is fixed by Σ_c a_k(c) δ[k, c] = 0 with `a_k(c) = mean_e h_k t_e(c)`; then δ[k, c] is the first-order change of
ln P.

**Curvature and the choice of metric.**

    ∂²NLL_e/∂δ[k,c]∂δ[k′,c′] = h_k h_k′ { W_e [diag(t_e) − t_e t_eᵀ] − Σ_i w_i [diag(q_i) − q_i q_iᵀ] }

The second bracket is a covariance matrix, so the hit term is concave in δ and the exact curvature H is bounded by
the normaliser's: `H ≼ mean_e (h hᵀ)_e ⊗ C_e`, `C_e = diag(t_e) − t_e t_eᵀ`. Two further steps keep the order:
`h hᵀ ≼ diag(h)` (lumping the knots), and `Σ_e h_k t_e t_eᵀ ≽ a_k a_kᵀ / w_k` (Cauchy–Schwarz, `w_k = Σ_c a_k(c)`).
Hence

    H ≼ F̃ = blockdiag_k [ diag(a_k) − a_k a_kᵀ / w_k ]        (the multinomial metric of the softmax)

On gauge-fixed vectors `F̃_k⁺ g = g / a_k`, so for the second-order model

    min_δ [g·δ + ½ δᵀHδ] ≤ − ½ Σ_k Σ_c g_k(c)² / a_k(c)                                   (Pearson form)

The multinomial metric is chosen because (a) it bounds the curvature from above for every δ direction, so the value is
a **lower bound on the gain of the best small perturbation**; (b) it inverts in closed form with the gauge handled
exactly; (c) it equals, to second order, the gain of one EM step that replaces t by d inside each knot. A
Fisher-diagonal metric would ignore the gauge; the exact Hessian is ill-conditioned (below).

**Estimator.** Squared sample gradients are dominated by noise (in-sample value 117 against 7.2 cross-validated). The
events are cut into 200 random blocks; with block sums x_b of `h_k G_e`,

    U = Σ_{b ≠ b′} x_bᵀ M x_b′ / Σ_{b ≠ b′} n_b n_b′ ,        gain = U / 2

uses only products of gradients from disjoint events and is unbiased for γᵀMγ (γ the population gradient). SE:
delete-one-block jackknife. It is the average of ½ g_Aᵀ M g_B over all half-splits; 400 random half-splits give the
same mean. Cells with a_k(c) < 10⁻⁸ w_k are left unperturbed (no effect: floor 10⁻⁶ → 7.17, floor 0 → 7.19).

**What the test bounds.**
- The gain of the *best small perturbation* of `mlp_std` among θ-blind 128 × 32 maps with six momentum knots, in the
  population limit, is at least 7.2 ± 3.1 and, in the resolved directions, 12.5 ± 4.2. A gain of 30 from such
  directions is 4 SE away. The implied step is small (rms change of ln P 4 %, 11 % at the lowest knot), so the
  second-order statement is self-consistent.
- It localises the headroom: p ≲ 4 GeV, the entrance, cell-scale structure. For p ≥ 6 GeV (70 % of the events) the
  MLP is at the optimum within about 1.

**What it does not bound.**
- It is local and first order (second order in the loss). Gains that need O(1) changes of ln P are outside it.
- Directions blurred by the 48.6 mm cell (λ < 0.25) are not bounded: no signal is seen, but the test has no power
  there.
- It ignores what a real fit needs. A free table cannot realise the headroom from 70k events (table above); only a
  structured, low-dimensional correction or an average over independent fits can. An ensemble *is* a single map, so
  its gain is part of this headroom, not in addition to it.
- More momentum resolution than six knots is not excluded (12 knots: 8.4 ± 4.6) and not supported.
- It says nothing about θ-dependent structure.

A consistent second estimate: the MLP's training-to-validation optimism exceeds the formula's by 13.0 ± 3.3
(training gap +35.0 ± 1.6, validation gap +22.0 ± 2.9). For a maximum-likelihood fit half of the optimism is the
estimation-variance cost on held-out data: about 6.5 ± 1.7 that averaging over fits could recover.

## 3. Part C — interpretation

+17.95 ± 2.51 (81 % of the gap); giving it the formula's z-marginal costs +4.58 ± 1.45 (21 %); interaction

ceiling A-L (−5 … −12) is too large.

### 3.2 D6a — Jensen bias, B2 and guard G-K

- c = 0.1604 ± 0.0035 (MLP) and 0.1610 ± 0.0036 (formula); at K = 64 both carry 25.1 of bias; the gap's coefficient
  is 0.0006 ± 0.0013.
- Why they agree: c is the relative variance of the density across a 48.6 × 48.6 × 20.5 mm detector cell. That is a
  property of the shower at the scale of the cell, which both models fit; a model can lower it only by deviating
  from the data at that scale.
- **B2.** If training at K = 16 had pushed the MLP towards smoother maps, its c would be below that of the nearly
  rigid formula and the gap would shrink with K. Measured: gap(16) − gap(1024) = +0.32 ± 0.88, i.e.
  c_F − c_MLP < 0.003 at 2 SE. In a quadratic trade-off the NLL that K = 64 training could recover is about
  ½ (1/16 − 1/64) Δc ≤ 0.7. **B2 is an expected null** (plan: 1–6). Finding 5 of PLAN §2 ("model-dependent,
  ≈ 1×10⁻⁴ between formula and MLP at K = 64") is not confirmed: 0.09 ± 0.21. Caveat: the formula was also trained
  at K = 16; the inference uses its rigidity (101 constants).
- **G-K.** It measures a difference of Jensen coefficients, and those are fixed by the physics for any model of
  normal sharpness. It will pass trivially for most candidates. It keeps discriminating power for candidates that
  change cell-scale sharpness: ensembles (averaging smooths), the smoothness pair, and anything evaluated on a
  refined lookup (Δc = +0.02 … +0.03, section 2.2).

### 3.3 D8 — ensembles

- Adding a weaker member to `mlp_std` hurts (+1.5 … +4.2): the Jensen gain of a pair is smaller than half the
  quality deficit of the weaker member (14–19).
- Against the *mean member* the gain is regular. With S the mean pairwise symmetric map KL of the members (12
  event-quantile momenta) and M the number of members, `gain = r (1 − 1/M) S / 4`:

  | ensemble | M | gain vs mean member | S [10⁻³] | r |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | L3xH16 + L3xH32 + L3xH64 | 3 | −10.25 ± 0.40 | 9.82 | 0.63 |
  | `mlp_std` + L3xH32 | 2 | −4.05 ± 0.19 | 5.80 | 0.56 |
  | `mlp_std` + L3xH16 | 2 | −5.84 ± 0.28 | 9.32 | 0.50 |
  | `mlp_std` + L3xH64 | 2 | −5.37 ± 0.26 | 6.90 | 0.62 |
  | L3xH16 + L3xH32 | 2 | −7.19 ± 0.40 | 11.16 | 0.52 |
  | L3xH16 + L3xH64 | 2 | −8.01 ± 0.21 | 9.60 | 0.67 |
  | L3xH32 + L3xH64 | 2 | −7.93 ± 0.47 | 8.69 | 0.73 |
  | all four | 4 | −9.62 ± 0.39 | 8.58 | 0.60 |

  r = 0.60 ± 0.08 (sd over the eight). r is the share of the map-level mixing gain (1 − 1/M) S / 4 that survives
  the detector folding; it plays the role of the visible fraction, which is 0.26 for the formula–MLP difference.
- **Prediction for five `mlp_std`-quality networks.** An additive fit of the six pairwise KLs gives each network a
  deviation from the consensus: `mlp_std` 2.4, L3xH64 4.0, L3xH32 4.3, L3xH16 6.5 (×10⁻³; residuals ≤ 0.9). Five
  networks like `mlp_std` then have S ≈ 4.9×10⁻³ and

      gain_5 vs mean member = 0.60 × 0.8 × 4.9×10⁻³ / 4 = −5.9   (range −3.5 … −8.5: r ± 13 %, S ± 40 %)

  Pre-registered check, data-free, as soon as the five networks exist: measure S and compare the E5 gain with
  0.12 S. Against `mlp_std` itself the gain is this number plus (mean member − `mlp_std`). Probability that B1 meets
  the promotion rule (≤ −5 and ≤ −3 SE against `mlp_std`): about 50 %.
- Three independent estimates of the single-map headroom now agree: score test ≥ 7.2 ± 3.1, optimism difference
  6.5 ± 1.7, ensemble extrapolation 5.9.

### 3.4 D4 / D5 — what both models miss, and what a θ-blind model can reach

**Entry-region pulls (±20–34) are mostly an artefact of the D4 premise.** D4 compares hit centres with samples that
are folded with a cell placed independently of the shower frame. The frame origin *is* the centroid of the first hit

1.9 ± 0.8 to a θ-blind map (D7), against a face value of 191 from the D4 shares of z′ ∈ [−20, 35) mm
(½ Σ (obs − exp)²/exp over the coarse regions, noise-corrected).

**Reach of a θ-blind model, from the same D4 shares outside the origin block (z′ < 35 mm, r < 60 mm).**

Coarse regions, three θ classes, so these are lower bounds; they show the order: **what both models miss is θ

**The cause is geometric: lateral faces (`checks/c_theta.py`, `c_edge.py`, `c_side.py`).**

## 4. Part D — pre-registered run lists for Milestone 2

### 4.0 Rules common to both tracks

- **Harness.** `scripts/fit.py` + `sfm.train.fit`; registry evaluation = unmodified `common.test_nll` on validation
  (K = 64, seed 12345) inside `deterministic()`. Selection of every new fit: last iterate, validation at K = 64.
- **Pairing.** Every Δ is paired by event against the comparator trained with the same seed.
- **Keep rule, Track A** (PLAN §5): Δ ≤ −3 and ≤ −2 SE against the base. **Promotion, Track B** (PLAN §6):
  Δ ≤ −5 and ≤ −3 SE against `mlp_std`, better than B0 by > 1 SE, guards passed.
- **Replication** (both tracks): a result counts only if the second seed gives Δ ≤ half the threshold with the same
  sign and the mean of the two seeds meets the threshold.
- **Equal NLL** (for "cleaner" candidates): Δ within +1 paired SE and below +1 on both seeds.
- **Convergence.** A run has converged if the validation mean moved by less than 0.5 over its last 1000 steps. A
  non-converged primary is rerun once with doubled steps together with its comparator; nothing else changes.
- **Guards** (PLAN §6, §13) are applied to kept Track A rungs as well: G-K (K = 1024), G-bulk on `zb` ≥ 1221 mm
  with `Bulk(short="raise")`, G-region, G-mass. Reason: 46 % of the formula–MLP gap is in approximate-score cells.
- **Secondary columns, pre-registered here** (never selection criteria): Δ for p < 2 GeV and p ≥ 2 GeV; Δ for
  contained and side-leaking events (`checks/c_side.py::exit_depth`).
- **At most six primaries per track**, listed below; everything else is exploratory and is reported as such.
- **If the budget runs out**, runs are dropped in the order given at the end of each list; primaries are never
  dropped.

`front` = mean_e A_front (exists); `stored` = mean_e A_front∧back (exists);
`back_corrected` = mean_e A_front∧back / mean_e 1[z′ < zb_e] — the back face enters once, through `M_e`;
`full_box` = mean_e A_front∧back∧side / mean_e 1[z′ < zb_e]. The last two need two new `kind`s in
`acceptance_template`.

## 5. Limits of this review

- Not recomputed: D1 strata, D4 pull tables, D9 spectra of the audit; the families author's data-free KL values.
- The lateral-face geometry is inferred from the hit distribution with a straight-line origin; 1.35 % of the events
  do not fit it. The first-order acceptance gains are sizes, not predictions of fitted arms.
- D6(c) uses the legacy coefficients, fitted through the centre rule; a refitted exact model may behave differently.
- D7 is 2.3 SE from zero by the jackknife (p < 0.003 by the sign-flip null). Its exact-curvature variant reuses the
  validation events for the curvature and the gradient.
- The ensemble prediction rests on four networks of three architectures; S for `mlp_std`-quality networks is an
  extrapolation (± 40 %).
- Probabilities in the run lists are judgements.
